Sources, definitions and interpretation

Source: Central Bank of Ireland; Fiscal Council estimates based on Carroll, Estimating monthly consumer spending using card payment data (PDF).

This dashboard presents estimates of monthly consumer spending in Ireland, combining Central Bank of Ireland card and cash payment data with Central Statistics Office (CSO) rent data. CSO and Eurostat price indices are used to estimate real spending. These are modelled indicators, rather than an official national accounts release. Data from March 2023 onwards are available.

Reading the growth measures. By default, growth charts compare the sum of spending over three months with the same three months a year earlier. Turn off the rolling-sum option to see monthly year-on-year growth. The summary cards show the measures stated on their labels. In the explorer, select the measure directly from the display menu.

Levels and composition. Nominal spending is measured at current prices; real spending adjusts for price changes. Real levels are shown in deflated euros. The composition chart uses nominal levels, with services included in the durability breakdown to show total consumption. COICOP is the Classification of Individual Consumption According to Purpose. An uncategorised residual is included.

Comparisons and coverage. The estimates are not seasonally adjusted. Year-on-year comparisons help account for recurring seasonal patterns. Growth in a three-month sum is a comparison with a year earlier, rather than with the preceding three months. Each breakdown uses its own price adjustments, so real aggregates may differ. Growth rates are not additive contributions.

Exploring and downloading. Date filters apply to their respective charts; headline indicators always refer to the latest available headline month. Monthly growth calculated from levels requires a year of earlier observations; rolling growth requires two additional months. Missing values are omitted and lines break at gaps. Tables provide exact values and download buttons save the displayed data. This dashboard contains a fixed data release and does not update automatically.
